Video Game Forecasting

This project analyzes historical video game sales data to identify the platform, genre, review, and regional factors that influenced game performance and to support 2017 marketing recommendations for an online game store.

Using a dataset of 16,715 games, I cleaned and prepared the data, explored platform life cycles, compared critic and user review impact, analyzed genre and regional sales differences, and tested key rating hypotheses to determine which segments were most relevant for future advertising decisions.

Project Goal

The goal of this analysis was to determine which platforms, genres, and regions showed the strongest sales potential so marketing resources could be allocated more effectively for the 2017 campaign period.

Tools Used

Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, SciPy, Exploratory Data Analysis, Statistical Analysis, Hypothesis Testing

What I Did

  • Cleaned and transformed 16,715 game records by standardizing columns, converting data types, and preparing sales fields for analysis.
  • Analyzed platform trends from 2012 to 2016 to identify the most relevant platforms for forward-looking campaign decisions.
  • Compared critic and user review scores to evaluate how strongly each related to game sales performance.
  • Explored regional differences across North America, Europe, and Japan to uncover market-specific platform and genre preferences.
  • Performed hypothesis testing to assess whether average user ratings differed across platforms and genres.

Key Findings

  • PS4, XOne, WiiU, 3DS, and PSV emerged as the most relevant platforms for future campaign targeting.
  • Critic scores were more useful than user scores for predicting Xbox 360 sales.
  • Action, Shooter, and Sports were among the strongest genres overall, while Japan showed different platform and genre preferences from North America and Europe.
  • The analysis supported data-driven recommendations for platform targeting, genre focus, and regional ad strategy.
