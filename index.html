Video Game Forecasting

Forecasting analysis project identifying the platform, genre, and regional factors that influenced video game sales and supported 2017 campaign recommendations.

Tools Used

Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, SciPy, Statistical Analysis, Hypothesis Testing

Key Results

  • Cleaned and analyzed 16,715 video game records.
  • Identified PS4, XOne, WiiU, 3DS, and PSV as the most relevant platforms.
  • Found critic scores were more predictive of sales than user scores.
  • Uncovered major regional differences across North America, Europe, and Japan.
